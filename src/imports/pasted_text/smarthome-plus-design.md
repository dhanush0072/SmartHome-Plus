Design a premium Apple-inspired Smart Home Automation web application called "SmartHome+".

The entire application should feel like an official Apple product, inspired by Apple Home, Apple Health, Apple Weather, Apple Shortcuts, Apple Settings, visionOS, and macOS Tahoe.

The interface must be elegant, minimal, luxurious, and extremely polished. Avoid cyberpunk, gaming aesthetics, neon lighting, dark futuristic themes, or heavy gradients.

Use Apple's Human Interface Guidelines throughout the design.
